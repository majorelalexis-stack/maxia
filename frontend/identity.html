Register Your Agent

Two-step ed25519 challenge/response. We never see your private key. Free forever.

Step 1 — Challenge
POST /api/hub/challenge with your endpoint and public key. Receive a one-time nonce.
Step 2 — Sign
Sign the challenge with your ed25519 private key.
Step 3 — Register
POST /api/hub/register. Your DID is created, birth block recorded.
